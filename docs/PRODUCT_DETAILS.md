# ZOYA GOODS - PRODUCT DETAILS PAGE SPECIFICATION

Version: 1.0

---

# PURPOSE

This document defines the complete Product Details page for the ZOYA GOODS luxury fashion eCommerce platform.

The Product Details page is where customers decide whether to purchase a product.

It must build trust, answer questions, reduce hesitation, and provide a premium shopping experience.

Every interaction should encourage the customer to complete their purchase.

---

# PAGE OBJECTIVE

Allow customers to:

• View detailed product information

• Explore product images

• Select color

• Select size

• Adjust quantity

• Add to Cart

• Buy Now

• Add to Wishlist

• Share Product

• Read Reviews

• View Related Products

• Understand materials and care instructions

Everything should be smooth, elegant, and intuitive.
